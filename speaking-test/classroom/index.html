Examiner reminders

  • Part I: ask the five questions in order; neutral follow-ups such as “Why?” or “Can you give an example?” are permitted.
  • Part II: both candidates should contribute to a natural exchange and work toward a practical outcome.
  • Use consistent timing and instructions for all candidates.
  • Judge pronunciation by intelligibility, not imitation of a native accent.
